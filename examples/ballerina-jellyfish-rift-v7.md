# Part A — English Copy-Ready Prompt

Create an 11-second fixed-camera cinematic one-take video.

A beautiful young East Asian ballerina stands waist-up in a warm Victorian flower conservatory. She has long glossy black hair, a small ivory flower above one ear, clear brown eyes, natural makeup, and an elegant ivory rehearsal dress. White roses, brass greenhouse arches, and soft golden sunset light remain stable behind her. Both hands are visible. Everything outside the dimensional effect remains the same real woman, clothing, lighting, and conservatory throughout.

Create one opaque horizontal dimensional image slice floating across her upper chest and face area. Alternate-world imagery fills the slice edge to edge. It is a broad, shallow, sharp-edged rectangle, approximately 74% of frame width and 26% of frame height. It has two straight horizontal long edges, short straight ends, crisp corners, and only a thin cyan–magenta seam along its edge. The real background cannot be seen through the slice. It is not glass and has no transparent surface or thick frame.

Inside the slice, show a large head-and-shoulders view of the same woman transformed into a beautiful deep-sea jellyfish oracle. The alternate face stays large and clearly visible inside the slice. Her luminous sapphire eyes, pearl-white skin, nose, mouth, cheeks, long dark hair, broad translucent jellyfish fins, crescent pearl crown, and indigo-opal collar appear together as one coherent character. The alternate environment remains visible to the left and right of her face: a vivid cobalt abyssal palace with drifting jellyfish lights. Use bold Art Nouveau illustration, clean dark contours, pearlescent blue gradients, and large readable shapes. The oracle mirrors the ballerina's gaze, expression, head tilt, and hand rhythm. Keep the alternate-world imagery clearly visible from about 0.6s until about 10.0s.

Art Nouveau is only the painted appearance of the alternate image. The slice itself is not physically made from glass, paper, fabric, mosaic, metal, ceramic, or any object. It is opaque, flat, taut, image-filled, and has no material thickness.

The complete visual footprint, including glow and folded area, never exceeds 25% of the frame. Keep all glow thin and tight against the edge. Do not let the slice grow taller than about 28% of the frame.

Her hands remain near the left and right ends. Fingers actively pull, spread, tilt, twist, and release. The slice responds instantly and elastically. Her palms never support it from below, and she never holds it like a card.

[0.0–1.0s] A thin cyan–magenta seam between her lateral hands instantly opens into the full 74% × 26% image-filled rectangle. The large oracle face and cobalt palace appear immediately.

[1.0–3.0s] Hold the hero image. The oracle's complete face, crown, jellyfish fins, collar, and surrounding underwater palace remain bright, coherent, and easy to read. The real conservatory outside remains unchanged.

[3.0–4.8s] Her left hand pulls slightly toward camera while her right hand eases back. The slice becomes a mild horizontal trapezoid; the oracle face remains large and intact while the underwater palace gains gentle depth.

[4.8–6.6s] Opposing hand motion creates one shallow off-center elastic fold and a soft horizontal wave. The slice stays broad, connected, opaque, and filled with the coherent oracle image. It never becomes a diamond, triangle, vertical crack, or empty outline.

[6.6–9.6s] Both hands lift and gently tilt the entire slice across the real face, like the target image passing in front of her. The large oracle face remains visible. One bright cyan scan light sweeps once from left to right inside the alternate image, illuminating the oracle eyes and crown; the full image-filled slice remains open and never collapses into a bare light line.

[9.6–11.0s] The slice snaps back into a flat horizontal rectangle. Hold the complete oracle face until 10.0s, then shrink directly into the thin cyan–magenta seam and close between the approaching fingertips. The ballerina lowers her hands and gives a calm, beautiful smile.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

NO transparent eye shield. NO clear glass panel. NO oval lens. NO ellipse. NO capsule. NO eye-shaped aperture. NO curved long edges. NO vertical black crack. NO centered vertical seam. NO vertical slit. NO isolated eye. NO floating lips. NO detached face. NO chest-level portrait. NO blank strip. NO empty light line before 10.0s. NO HUD. NO interface. NO screen frame. NO physical card. NO book. NO butterfly. NO hands cupping from below. NO edge gripping. NO duplicate person. NO miniature full-body figure. NO picture-in-picture. NO recursive image. NO full-frame alternate takeover. NO changes to the real woman outside the slice. NO text, logo, subtitle, cut, zoom, pan, orbit, excessive rays, sparks, lightning, or particles.

# Part B — 中文执行版

生成一段11秒、固定机位、电影质感、一镜到底的视频。

一位年轻漂亮的东方芭蕾舞者站在暖色维多利亚花房中，腰部以上构图。她留着柔亮黑色长发，耳侧别一朵小巧象牙白花，棕色眼睛清澈，妆容自然，身穿优雅的象牙白练功裙。背景固定为白玫瑰、黄铜温室拱架和柔和金色夕阳。双手完整入画。次元效果之外的真人、服装、光线和花房全程保持不变。

生成一块悬浮在她胸口上方与脸部前方的横向次元影像切片。异世界影像不透明、鲜艳，并一直铺满到每条边。整体是一块宽阔扁平、边缘锐利的横向矩形，约占画面宽度74%、高度26%；上下长边笔直水平，两端短边笔直，四角清晰，边缘只保留一圈很细的青—洋红色差光缝。透过切片绝对看不到现实花房。它不是玻璃，没有透明表面，也没有粗重框体。

切片内部显示同一名舞者变成深海水母神谕者后的大尺寸半身近景。异世界脸必须足够大、第一眼就能看清。发光蓝宝石眼睛、珍珠白皮肤、鼻子、嘴唇、面颊、深色长发、两片宽阔半透明水母鳍、珍珠弯月头冠和靛蓝欧泊衣领同时组成一个完整连续的人物。脸部左右保留鲜明的钴蓝深海宫殿与漂浮水母灯。采用大色块新艺术插画、干净深色轮廓、珍珠蓝渐变和清楚醒目的造型。神谕者与真人保持相同的视线、表情、头部倾斜和手势节奏。

新艺术风格只代表异世界影像的绘制效果。切片本身不是玻璃、纸张、布料、马赛克、金属、陶瓷或任何实体物体。它不透明、平整、绷紧、内部充满影像，没有实体厚度。

裂隙包括边缘光和折叠区域在内的完整视觉面积，每帧都不得超过画面25%。所有边缘光保持细窄并紧贴切片，切片高度不超过画面约28%。

双手始终位于切片左右两端附近，手指持续做拉开、张开、倾斜、扭转和释放动作。切片对手势即时弹性响应。掌心绝不从下方托住切片，也不能像拿卡片一样抓着它。

[0.0–1.0秒] 双手之间一条细青—洋红色差缝迅速展开成宽74%、高26%、内部充满异世界影像的完整矩形。大尺寸神谕者脸与钴蓝宫殿立即出现。

[1.0–3.0秒] 保持主体展示。神谕者完整的脸、头冠、水母鳍、衣领和左右深海宫殿持续清楚、鲜艳、稳定可读。切片外的现实花房完全不变。

[3.0–4.8秒] 左手略向镜头拉近，右手略向后送，切片形成轻微横向梯形。神谕者脸仍然大而完整，深海宫殿产生柔和景深。

[4.8–6.6秒] 双手反向动作产生一次浅幅偏心弹性折叠和柔和横向波动。切片始终宽阔、连通、不透明，内部保持完整神谕者影像；绝不能变成菱形、三角形、竖直黑缝或空心轮廓。

[6.6–9.6秒] 双手抬高并轻轻倾斜整块切片，让它从真人脸前经过。大尺寸神谕者脸持续可见。一道明亮青色扫描光只在异世界影像内部从左向右扫过一次，照亮神谕者的眼睛和头冠；完整影像切片始终展开，绝不能缩成单独光线。

[9.6–11.0秒] 切片弹回平整横向矩形，完整神谕者脸保持到10.0秒后，才直接收缩成细青—洋红色差缝并在靠近的指尖之间闭合。舞者放下双手，露出平静漂亮的微笑。
