# Local Dual-World Moving-Matte Lessons

## Base plate first

- Establish the real person and real scene before describing the alternate world.
- Keep at least 75% of every frame recognizably real.
- Outside the slit, 100% of pixels remain the original base plate.
- Alternate materials and architecture have zero visibility outside the moving matte.

## One body, local replacement

- Render one subject, one head, one body, one pose.
- When the slit crosses the eyes, replace only the covered eye band.
- When it crosses clothing, replace only the covered clothing band.
- Restore every fragment immediately after the slit passes.
- Do not compose an alternate portrait, complete character, inset shot, recursive image, or miniature person inside the slit.

## Rift geometry

- Long shallow horizontal cut; no frame body or transparent margin.
- Single-pixel cyan–magenta chromatic seam.
- Complete envelope remains inside 78% × 22% and at or below 25% total area.
- One off-center diagonal crease creates two unequal planes.
- No centered spine, symmetric V, matching wings, butterfly, or electronic book.

## Hands

- Keep visible air gaps between hands and slit.
- Avoid gripping matching corners or holding a rectangular framing pose.
- Pass one hand in front while the other remains behind.
- Use asymmetrical, causal gestures throughout.
