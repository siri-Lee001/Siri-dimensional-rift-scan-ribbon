# Borderless Dimensional Image-Slit Lessons

## Appearance

- Long shallow horizontal image replacement
- Alternate imagery fills every pixel to the boundary
- Single-pixel cyan-magenta chromatic seam
- No transparent glass margin, frame body, screen, HUD, or interface graphics

## Geometry

- Complete envelope stays inside 78% × 22%
- Total visible footprint remains ≤25%
- One off-center diagonal crease creates two unequal planes
- No centered spine, symmetric V, matching wings, butterfly, or electronic book

## Subject

- Exactly one life-size alternate face-and-upper-torso crop
- Same face scale, gaze, expression, head angle, pose, and gestures
- No miniature full-body figure, distant hangar character, multiple mecha, or complete robot

## Hands

- Keep visible air gaps between hands and slit
- Avoid gripping matching corners
- Pass one hand in front while the other remains behind to create depth
- Use asymmetric gestures throughout
