# Geometric Holographic Rift Lessons

## Correct material model

- Thin, crisp cyan-magenta-violet energy perimeter
- Flat, smooth, taut, semi-transparent holographic membrane
- Straight edges and sharp corners in the open state
- Hard crease lines and planar acute facets when folded
- Alternate imagery on every visible face; no black backface
- Local colored light spill on fingers and cheeks

## Area calculation

- Limit the sum of all screen-space projected rift surfaces to ≤25% of the full frame on every frame.
- Treat 78% width × 28% height as approximately 21.84% area.
- When folded or split, add every visible facet; do not grant each facet its own allowance.

## Six-beat behavior

1. Activate a 2–3 pixel energy line.
2. Open a legal sharp rectangular holographic window.
3. Push one side in depth and perform one controlled shear.
4. Form connected acute facets along hard diagonal creases.
5. Reopen and scan across the face while the alternate person mirrors the subject.
6. Snap flat, contract to the line, and close.

## Failure lessons

- Never use ribbon, black slit, fabric, silk, scarf, accordion, flutter, or soft-fold language.
- Hands stay beside the lateral edges or upper corners and control invisible tension; they do not hold a physical object.
- The alternate person mirrors gaze, expression, head angle, pose, and gestures in real time.
- Do not prescribe an output ratio.
