# Example: Localized Slice Behavior

Use this as a behavioral target, not a fixed character template.

## Recommended high-contrast input

- Duration: 11 seconds
- Real scene: cold, desaturated night environment
- Real character: beautiful live-action subject in restrained dark clothing
- Slice identity: luminous ceremonial or mythic version of the same subject
- Art style: mineral-pigment mural, mother-of-pearl lacquer, stained glass, or gold-leaf painting
- Ending: a small smile, flower petal, finger heart, or restrained thumbs-up

## Required 8-beat behavior

1. Pinch a 1–2% high slit
2. Pull open a 16–20% localized ribbon
3. Move it up and down; uncovered regions restore immediately
4. Form a connected three-panel accordion with two diagonal creases
5. Split into three thin horizontal slices with live-action gaps
6. Rejoin and perform one shallow S-sweep
7. Compress to slit and point
8. Finish as the original live-action person

## Lessons from forward testing

- Alternate identity must exist only inside the currently visible ribbon.
- Do not let scanned areas remain transformed.
- Do not combine full-body identity replacement, armor, crown, lightning, axial twist, and double scan in one 11-second prompt.
- Energy-line language can collapse the effect into a laser or light-sword gesture; use a dark slit and borderless image slice instead.
- First-pass tests should change art material and costume details inside the slice while preserving the original silhouette.
- Add more radical species or body changes only after the slice mechanism works reliably.

## Validation target

- Timeline beats: 8
- Exact local-only phrase: present
- Immediate restoration: present
- Full-body and cumulative transformation guardrails: present
- Slit, active ribbon, and hard maximum sizes: present
- Three-panel fold and three-strip split: present
- Validator status: `PASS`
