# Gesture–Ribbon Action Library

Select complete units. Do not combine two medium/high-risk units in the same beat.

| ID | Real action | Ribbon response | Alternate response | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 Open | Forearms pull apart, fingers unfurl | Short chest seam opens into one borderless ribbon | Corresponding torso/costume appears; no face | low |
| F2 Close | Forearms converge, fingers pinch air | Ribbon contracts to a short seam and vanishes | Current body zone compresses once | low |
| S1 Rise scan | Both forearms rise at fixed spacing | Flat ribbon rises chest -> neck -> face | Corresponding zones replace one another at fixed scale | low |
| S2 Return scan | Both forearms lower at fixed spacing | Flat ribbon lowers face -> neck -> chest | Corresponding zones return in reverse | low |
| S3 Lateral sweep | Forearms travel together left then right | Flat ribbon moves sideways | Fixed counterpart is revealed by position; no portrait travel | medium |
| S4 Diagonal sweep | One forearm leads, the other follows | Flat ribbon takes one shallow diagonal path | Corresponding body zones change continuously | medium |
| P1 Depth tilt | One wrist advances, the other recedes | Flat ribbon becomes a shallow trapezoid then restores | Current region keeps scale | medium |
| P2 Elastic pulse | Fingers flex and release beside ends | Width pulses once then settles | Visible organ gives one response | low |
| G1 Bow-tie fold | Both wrists rotate inward, elbows stay wide, hands stay outside | Two joined diagonal facets form; inner acute tips meet; brief hold; full flatten | One continuous image bends in depth without duplication | high |

## Rules

- Start with F1 and end with F2.
- Scan only while the ribbon is fully flat.
- G1 owns its beat; do not move the ribbon during folding.
- After G1, flatten completely before S2, S3, or S4.
- Change hand shape between units: unfurl -> curved control -> inward wrist roll -> outward wrist release -> convergence.
- Repeat a unit only with changed direction or body zone.
