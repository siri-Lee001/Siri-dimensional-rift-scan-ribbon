# Scan-Ribbon Self-Check

Fix every failed item before delivery.

## Structure

- [ ] No `{...}` placeholder remains.
- [ ] English and Chinese versions use the same timing and effect mechanics.
- [ ] Timeline contains 9 continuous beats: line, pull, up-scan, reverse scan, S-wave, compact fold, double scan, axial twist/recovery, close.
- [ ] English prompt is approximately 900–1600 words for 11 seconds; no adjective-only padding.

## Target-effect fidelity

- [ ] Exact phrase `borderless body-registered transformation ribbon` appears.
- [ ] Ribbon intersects the subject's body plane; it does not float as a screen in front.
- [ ] Transformed face, hair, clothing, hands, and body remain locked to the real coordinates.
- [ ] Scan passage converts regions; reverse passage restores them.
- [ ] Background stays live-action and unchanged.
- [ ] No complete alternate-world landscape is presented as a separate window.

## Geometry and motion

- [ ] Ribbon height is stated as 8–18% of frame height, usually 12–14%.
- [ ] Ribbon area is ≤22% of frame area and width stays within arm span.
- [ ] Ribbon is borderless: no neon rectangle, picture frame, doorway, screen bezel, or portal frame.
- [ ] Fold stays a compact low-height three-panel accordion with two diagonal creases.
- [ ] Fold never becomes a diamond, giant butterfly, card, paper sheet, or film strip.
- [ ] Axial twist uses the full ribbon but remains limited and low-height.
- [ ] No idle beat exceeds 0.25 seconds.

## Anatomy and identity

- [ ] `SAME PERSON` means synchronization and coordinate registration, not identical costume or species.
- [ ] One continuous person only; no duplicate body, separate transformed character, split screen, or double exposure.
- [ ] Hands keep five fingers, continuous wrists, and correct partial transformation when crossing the ribbon.
- [ ] The face is never completely hidden; identity remains readable.

## Style and safety

- [ ] Artistic style comes from user input or `preset-dict.md`; no ordinary anime default.
- [ ] English is imperative technical language; Chinese is native storyboard language.
- [ ] No copyrighted character/franchise names.
- [ ] No text, logo, watermark, subtitle, UI, flashing, or random camera movement.
