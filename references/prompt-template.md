# Concise Borderless Ribbon Template

Author both languages independently. For a five-second fold test, use five beats and keep the English prompt between 260 and 430 words.

## English

```text
Create a {DURATION}-second fixed frontal-camera photorealistic one-take video.

REAL: {REAL_SUBJECT} in {REAL_SCENE}. Keep the person and room unchanged outside the effect. Both hands and forearms remain visible.

EFFECT: One opaque borderless horizontal dimensional scan ribbon, about {WIDTH}% of frame width and {HEIGHT}% of frame height, never over 25% complete footprint. It has straight hard cut boundaries and no visible outline, frame, glow, glass, or thickness. Both hands remain outside its ends with clear air gaps.

REGISTERED COUNTERPART: One {ALTERNATE_CHARACTER}, rendered in {ART_STYLE}, occupies the same apparent body position, scale, pose, and facing direction. The ribbon replaces only the body zone it crosses. Its local background is the corresponding alternate version of the same immediate space, not a separate framed vista. Subject silhouette continues through both horizontal boundaries.

TIMELINE:
{DURATION_PROFILE}

NEGATIVE: NO visible border or frame; NO monitor, card, glass, or portal; NO oversized face or traveling portrait; NO hand entering or covering the ribbon; NO gripping or supporting; NO duplicate subject; NO detached fold pieces; NO center crack; NO text, logo, cut, zoom, or pan.
```

Every beat uses:

```text
[start–end]
Real action: joint-led hand trajectory and finger-state change.
Ribbon response: one main motion or deformation.
Alternate response: corresponding visible body region and synchronized action.
```

## Chinese

Use natural visible language. Prefer “无边界横向局部异世界重绘带” over “屏幕、窗口、面板、卡片”. Keep hands permanently outside both ends. If folding is requested, describe one full fold-and-flatten beat and do not add other motion inside it.
